Krown Properties is a residential development, investment, and advisory platform operating across Auckland. Since its establishment in 2015, the company has delivered a diverse portfolio of projects ranging from boutique architectural townhouses to large-scale residential communities and build-to-rent developments.

Working alongside leading architects, planners, and investment partners, Krown Properties focuses on creating high-quality housing that combines thoughtful urban design, strong financial discipline, and long-term investment value.
