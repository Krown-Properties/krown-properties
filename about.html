About Us

Founded on One
Principle

Founded in 2015, Krown Properties was built on one principle — that quality residential development should be accessible without compromise.


We are a vertically integrated property developer and construction company, controlling every stage of the build process to deliver homes that stand apart from the rest.


From site acquisition and resource consent to construction and handover, our team brings decades of combined experience in Auckland's complex development landscape. We specialise in multi-dwelling projects, townhouse developments, and large-scale subdivisions across the greater Auckland region.
