About KPPM

Expert Management
Rooted in Development

Based in Auckland, Krown Properties is a leading residential development and property management firm. We specialise in multi-dwelling and build-to-rent housing, providing quality solutions to the Auckland market since 2015.


Unlike general property managers, KPPM's management expertise is rooted directly in development. We understand the assets we manage because in many cases, we built them — giving us an unparalleled understanding of the buildings, their Body Corp requirements, maintenance needs, and investment performance.


We also manage a growing number of private residential properties for individual landlords, applying the same rigorous standards and processes developed across our own portfolio.
